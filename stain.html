STAIN — animated short

"In a world where everything is done for you, and creativity is non-existent, a simple stain can be a sign of agency."

Exhibited at Moezeum, Rotterdam. Open in Google Maps? — alongside the screening, visitors were invited to make their own stains, engaging their own creativity and imagination.

▶ Stain (2026) Animated Short — LUCY POK
