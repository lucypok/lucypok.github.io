DEPOP — spec campaign

The metro is fast, urban, and sustainable, just like Depop. Or what it aspires to be.

Depop is a peer-to-peer marketplace to buy, sell and discover secondhand fashion.

What they're missing is a direct line to the young, urban community that already fits that identity.

In the Netherlands, 80% of young people rely on public transport. While waiting, most people are on their phones but the only moment they look up is when the metro arrives.

That's the moment we grab their attention and link it directly to Depop. By putting stickers on the metro windows, we frame every outfit as a potential Depop buy or sell.
