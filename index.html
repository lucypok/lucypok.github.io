Break-App: break up with an app, the GDPR way

Break-App helps you break up with apps and websites such as Instagram, Netflix, Amazon, TikTok and Spotify. Paste the name of an app or website to see what it knows about you, switch off tracking and marketing, object to how your data is used, or delete your account and data. You get a ready-to-send GDPR letter for each option. Made with Bits of Freedom.
