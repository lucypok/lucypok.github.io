HyperRaar — Subverting Instagram

Turn the doomscroll into music; together.

@hyperraar is a project responding to how we experience music today; through fragmented clips on social media, where we recognize parts of songs without ever hearing the whole.

With the Hyperraar mindset, we reframe the tools that keep us in the doomscroll: the endless looping, muting, fast-forwarding — and use them as a soundboard for making music. Each reel becomes a part. Alone, they feel incomplete but alongside each other they become something.

A Hyperraar only works when people come together.
No headphones. Just phones, out loud.

Project by Lucy, Noushin, Riats and Vicky
