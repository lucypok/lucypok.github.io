Roffa Mon Amour — Internship

Roffa Mon Amour is an open-air arthouse film festival in Rotterdam screening daring films, with events all through the year and a 12-day festival in August. My role was in the Marketing department.

From PR emails, writing film descriptions, managing the website, posts/reels, designing assets & stickers to giving feedback to AV first-year students at WDKA about their trailers.

The list is quite long and varied, as were my 6 months at RMA. However, everything clicked when I saw the festival come to life and the effect it had on people.
