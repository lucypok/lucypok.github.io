Hickeys, age and life imaginaries

(visual essay)

You can email me for the PDF version if you want to have a read :)

Summary:

While researching ageing and how society marks the ageing body, references to wrinkles, tattoos, and dead skin kept surfacing. I went looking for a mark that hadn't been explored as much, and landed on the hickey.

Hickeys sit in a strange cultural spot: intimate yet public, playful yet shameful, generally imagined as belonging to teenagers and young adults. I dug into what a hickey represents and who is allowed to wear one, testing my own initial reactions and the biases underneath them along the way. Research came together through academic texts, web and magazine articles, in-person interviews, and my own reflections.

I also experimented with AI image generation, placing the same hickey across different genders and ages, to see what kind of reactions it would provoke, including my own.

The zine was designed and made by me, with the cover printed on a faux leather material using WDKA's Mimaki printer.
